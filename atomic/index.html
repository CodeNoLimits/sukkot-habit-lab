THE BOOK IN A MINUTE

Design the routine,
then repeat the useful action.

The central approach is to improve the system around a behaviour: its cue, appeal, effort and immediate payoff. Repeated actions support your sense of who you are. Review the system as your needs change.

01 / CUEMake it obvious

Give the action a clear signal, time or place.

02 / CRAVINGMake it attractive

Connect the action with something appealing.

03 / RESPONSEMake it easy

Remove obstacles and shrink the starting step.

04 / REWARDMake it satisfying

Give the action a suitable immediate payoff.

For an unwanted habit: hide its cue, reduce its appeal, add effort and make its consequences noticeable. Official framework ↗

The “1% better” calculation is an illustration of compounding, not a promise of a particular personal result. Habit formation has no universal deadline.
