Known English translation issue at645:1: the Rema wording “totally separated” conflicts with the Hebrew “לא נפרדו”; the preference is for unseparated leaves. This guide’s summary does not repeat the error. The source transcript is preserved and the discrepancy flagged. The title/wording at641 also needs care: the established practice defers Shehecheyanu to festival Kiddush; it does not create a construction blessing.

The full source corpus is distinct from the independently written habit analogies. Atomic Habits by James Clear is not a halakhic source. No religious requirement is changed by an analogy, and no custom is represented as a universal obligation. Contemporary disputes and unusual structures require the relevant tradition and case.
